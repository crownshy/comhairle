Dear Delegates,

I have good news! It is time for you to onboard to Comhairle, where the Maine Citizens' Assembly Delegates Hub is located. Tonight we will make sure everyone is able to get in and look around, so take a moment to give it a try right before the meeting starts, if you can.

The Delegates Hub is where we will share materials with you about education and schooling in Maine so that you have a solid footing as you begin deliberations. You find a link to the Maine School Stories Portal, briefs written by us at MEPRI and CEPARE, and infographics and fact sheets from stakeholder organizations. It is designed to be worked though at your own pace and in your own way—and no quizzes will be given! It is designed as a resource to help you feel confident and to support all delegates in making informed decisions.

The Delegates Hub is also where the priority proposals will be drafted and where delegates will vote on those proposals.

If you have any technical questions about logging into Comhairle, please reach out to Andy Paice at CrownShy at andy@crown-shy.com
